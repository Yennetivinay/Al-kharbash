Company Overview

Al Kharbash Facility Management is an organization that understands analyses, strategizes and implements result oriented plan for your facilities to make them a mark of your success. We belie in quality based results oriented approach that continues to set us apart from the competition.

We continuously review, evaluate and update our staff’s technical competencies across every technical disciple in order to meet the needs of demanding customers. Our professional maintenance teams stay abreast with the latest developments in technology.

Group History

AL Kharbash Facilities Management comes under Al Kharbash Group of Companies, now in its fourth decade, a highly recognized and long standing U.A.E business group with a solid presence in Dubai. The group is wholly owned by the Kharbash family. It was founded by Mr. Abdulla Al Kharbash in 1970 and participates in a broad spectrum of industries.

Mr. Abdul Mutalib Abdulla Kharbash, the Managing Director of the group, is spearheading the operation of the company with the objective of excelling at all levels of its operations. The tremendous growth achieved over the past years is ascribed to his farsightedness and entrepreneurial skills, and to the company’s culture of keeping its customers satisfied.

The group has a number of sub divisions including Contracting, Electromechanical, Real Estate and Investments, Marketing and General Trading, General Maintenance, Cleaning and Security Services, with a heavy work force. Its current client base extends all the way back to its inception.
